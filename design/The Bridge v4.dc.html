ANSWERS TO THE PRD'S OPEN QUESTIONS
Progress orb → a literal bridgeThe outer span is the whole journey with a glowing orb marking you. The inner green arc fills with today's tasks. Light particles travel toward the orb so it moves even when idle.
Reality Check → tab inside TodayOne deliberate tap away, never on by default. The screen switches to a muted treatment with the image greyed out.
Photo skip → visible, secondaryA text button under the main CTA, with the trade-off stated in one line so nobody skips by accident.
Study Mode → full takeoverThe tab bar disappears and chat shrinks to a floating strip. Replies surface in a dismissible card, and the exchange is saved to the main thread.
Community → visible, unlocks at 7 daysThe tab is there from day one with a progress bar, so it reads as a reward instead of an empty room. You can turn the gate off in Tweaks.
Multiple goals → up to 3 at onceEach goal has its own bridge, plan, images and deadline. Switch from the pills on Today. One streak covers every goal, and the review step warns you when daily load passes 9 tasks.
Deadlines → picked by the userA date picker in step 1 with quick presets, at least a week out and no more than 5 years. Editable in Review and in Profile.
WHY PEOPLE COME BACK The streak only needs one task, so a bad day doesn't break it. Finishing every task reveals your Future Self full-screen. Journal milestones and badges mark progress over weeks. Image slots mark where the AI-generated images go. Drag art in to fill them.
